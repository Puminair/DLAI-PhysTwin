# Sensor tuning memo — reallocation + RF profiles

Scope: the CW9172I fleet (150 logical positions, pairs → 300 units) in the
Yochananof Centro Rehovot twin. Everything quantitative below is produced by
this repository's own physics; each number states its provenance.

> **Status:** the reallocation proposed here is now **executed**
> (`scripts/apply_reallocation.py`), and 3 operator Meraki units were added on
> top (`scripts/add_sensors.py`), taking the live fleet to **153 logical / 306
> units**. At 5 GHz this moved BOH ≥3-AP 66.9 → 74.8 → **80.7 %** and blind
> 17.2 → 6.1 → **4.6 %**. The memo below is the analysis that produced the
> proposal, against the 150 baseline.

## 1. The problem, with the real baseline numbers

Back of house has 28 logical sensors over 2,218 m² (config
`sensors.boh_units`, `areas.back_of_house_m2` — v0.2 quotas, under-provisioned
by design, CLAUDE.md §9). Baseline coverage at cart-panel height 1.00 m, 2 m
grid (provenance: `data/coverage_report.json`, generated by
`scripts/build_coverage.py` from `data/sensing_layer2.json` 1.0-parametric):

| band | sales ≥3 AP | sales blind | BOH ≥3 AP | BOH blind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.4 | 99.5 % | 0.5 % | 95.4 % | 3.7 % |
| 5 | 99.5 % | 0.5 % | **66.9 %** | **17.2 %** |
| 6 | 99.3 % | 0.5 % | 66.0 % | 17.8 % |

CLAUDE.md §8 is explicit: *no physics fixes it, only reallocation does.* The
donor pool is equally explicit: §9 says the 18 perimeter units cannot separate
inside from outside — a modem 8 m out in the courtyard is heard by 41 sensors
at −44 dBm — so they are not doing the job they were placed for.

## 2. Reallocation proposal — move 8 perimeter units into BOH gaps

Tool: `scripts/propose_reallocation.py` (K = 8 default, argparse). Placement
is a greedy heuristic — score each failing BOH cell position by how many
still-deficient cells sit within 12 m with a "line-of-sight-ish" path from
mount z = 3.0 m to panel z = 1.0 m (crossing set via
`world.geometry.segment_crossings`: no cold_room, ≤1 racking). The heuristic
only *orders* candidates; the effect is then **proved by re-running
`eval.coverage.coverage_grid`** — the same physics behind the README table —
on the modified sensor list. Numbers below are those re-runs (provenance:
`data/reallocation_proposal.json`, band 5, 2 m grid).

| zone | ≥3 AP before | ≥3 AP after | blind before | blind after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| back_of_house (326 cells) | 66.9 % | **74.8 %** | 17.2 % | **6.1 %** |
| sales_floor (564 cells) | 99.5 % | 99.3 % | 0.5 % | 0.5 % |

- **Donors** (8 of 18 perimeter units): S000, S001, S002, S013, S014, S015,
  S016, S017 — all on the sales side (x < 50.58 m split wall), preferring the
  west/south walls farthest from the split. Rationale: perimeter units east of
  the split sit over the sparse BOH field and actually help it; sales-side
  edge cells sit in front of 104 sensors over 2,782 m² (one per ~27 m²), so
  losing the ring there costs 0.2 points of sales ≥3 AP and nothing in blind.
- **New BOH positions** (z = 3.0 m, zone back_of_house): (89.0, 45.0),
  (89.0, 27.0), (83.0, 25.0), (87.0, 27.0), (83.0, 43.0), (99.0, 43.0),
  (99.0, 39.0), (83.0, 45.0). The clustering is deliberate: a blind cell needs
  *three* new links, and the greedy loop tracks per-cell deficit rather than
  marking a cell solved after one reach.
- Net effect at 5 GHz: blind BOH area cut ~3× (17.2 → 6.1 %), positionable
  BOH up 8 points, for zero hardware spend. The remaining ~25 % failing cells
  cluster around cold rooms (opaque — metal is a conductor, attenuation
  `null`) and deep racking rows; they need either more donors (`--k`) or the
  2.4 GHz RTLS policy below, which already covers BOH at 95.4 %.

Reproduce: `python3 scripts/propose_reallocation.py` (~30 s: two
coverage_grid runs plus the heuristic loop).

## 3. RF profiles (config/rf_profiles.yaml) — rationale

Full per-value provenance lives as comments in `config/rf_profiles.yaml`;
the load-bearing decisions:

- **RTLS band = 2.4 GHz, everywhere.** README table above: 2.4 GHz is the
  only band whose BOH coverage (95.4 %) survives the racking, because one
  crossing costs 17.7 dB instead of 33.4/36.7 dB (config `attenuation_db`,
  ITU-R P.2040-3). The 2 dB of extra antenna gain at 6 GHz does not begin to
  pay for a 19 dB worse wall.
- **20 MHz channel width at 2.4 GHz, channels 1/6/11** (verified Cisco
  practice — the only non-overlapping set; wider widths self-interfere).
- **TPC bounds tightened to ~11–17 dBm** (zone-specific: BOH floor 14, hence
  its sparser field; perimeter 8–14 to stop courtyard leakage). This tames
  the default RRM behaviour flagged in CLAUDE.md §5 and modelled in
  `sensing/sensor.py::rrm_tx_power_dbm`: Tx power swinging 2–26 dBm every
  30 min (config `sensors.rrm`). Note the honest caveat: Tx power is
  **downlink-only** — location comes from uplink frames, so this stabilises
  the cell plan and Air Marshal, it does not improve positioning.
- **Min bitrate 12 Mb/s** on the floor (6 in BOH) — assumed Cisco
  HD-deployment practice: prunes low-rate management floods off the air the
  RTLS uplink shares.
- **Band steering: clients to 5 GHz, scanning radio dedicated.** Staff
  phones/POS/handhelds go to 5 GHz so the 2.4 GHz air stays quiet for cart
  uplink frames; the CW9172I's dedicated scanning radio is never time-sliced
  for serving during trading.
- **Air Marshal auto-containment OFF during trading hours.** CLAUDE.md §5 gap
  table: containment time-splits the scanning radio and degrades RTLS
  accuracy. With five neighbour networks on this floor and above, containment
  without `wiredMacs` confirmation risks containing a neighbour's AP. DLAI
  stays RECOMMEND_ONLY (§6); containment is a human decision.

## 4. What this does NOT fix

- **Inside/outside discrimination stays broken.** Reallocation removes 8 of
  the 18 perimeter units; the remaining 10 still cannot separate a device in
  the courtyard from one at the gates (§9 — 41 sensors hear the 8 m-out modem
  at −44 dBm). No profile setting changes that. Do not build a feature that
  assumes a perimeter fence exists.
- **Gondola/racking attenuation is the most sensitive unmeasured parameter**
  (CLAUDE.md §9). 17.7/33.4/36.7 dB per crossing is computed from ITU-R
  P.2040-3 with an equivalent-thickness assumption; a loaded shelf is a steel
  back panel plus mixed goods, not a homogeneous dielectric. Every number in
  §2 inherits that assumption. **First thing to measure on site** — the
  values are runtime-overridable in config precisely so the re-run is one
  command.
- **Cold-room shadows remain.** Metal is a conductor (`null` = opaque);
  no sensor count fixes the geometry immediately behind them. Residual BOH
  blind cells after reallocation sit in these shadows.
- **The interior fit-out is parametric, not surveyed** (README provenance
  note): the proposed coordinates are relative to the modelled racking. On
  site, snap each of the 8 positions to the nearest clear ceiling point and
  re-run `scripts/propose_reallocation.py` against the surveyed geometry.
- **Cart panel Tx power is a class value** (config `cart_uplink`, assumed —
  no vendor figure). The entire uplink budget, and therefore every coverage
  percentage here, moves dB-for-dB with it.
