# Alert catalog — what each alert is built from

> **Generated from `config/alerts.yaml` by `scripts/gen_alert_docs.py`.**

> `tests/test_alerts.py` asserts every catalog id appears here.

This is the human companion to `config/alerts.yaml`. The alerts are
defined by **this building's context**, not generic IDS signatures: five
neighbour Wi-Fi networks share the floor, aWIPS is throttled and carries
no client identity, containment degrades RTLS floor-wide, RF cannot tell
inside from outside, and position flicker makes single fixes untrustworthy.

## Anatomy of an alert

Every `Alert` the engine emits (`dlai/alerts.py`) is:

| field | why it exists |
|---|---|
| `t_ms` | one clock, UTC ms — when the evidence timestamped, never a wall-clock read |
| `alert_id` | the catalog id that fired; the join key to this document |
| `severity` | `info` / `warn` / `high` — set by the catalog, not by the engine |
| `subject` | the thing the alert is about (a BSSID, an AP MAC, a cart MAC) |
| `action` | a **recommendation sentence** for a human operator — never an enforcement |
| `evidence` | the exact input fields the alert was constructed from — provenance |
| `confidence` | `observed` **only** for directly-heard facts (wire evidence, an aWIPS report); `inferred` for anything computed (a position, a duplicate, a silence). **An inference must never masquerade as an observation** — that is the whole discipline of the three-layer split. |
| `mode` | always `RECOMMEND_ONLY` — the PoC recommends, it does not act |
| `blind_spots` | what this alert **cannot know**: intensity lost to aWIPS throttling, inside/outside indiscriminable by RF, single fixes untrusted under flicker. Stated so an operator never over-reads the alert. |

**Deliberate non-alerts** (never added back): downlink RSSI anomalies
(RRM moves AP Tx power 2-26 dBm every 30 min — expected), and any claim
that a device is physically "inside" or "outside" the store on RF alone
(the 18 perimeter units cannot separate it).

## The alerts


_Mode: **RECOMMEND_ONLY**. Dedup window: `300000` ms (one alert per `alert_id`+`subject` per window)._


### `cart_mac_duplicate` — Cart panel MAC seen at two irreconcilable positions

- **Severity:** high  (implemented in the engine)
- **Source stream(s):** scanning_api, dlai_track
- **Built from:** two Scanning-API observations for one cart-OUI `clientMac`, each with a `cloud_location` (inferred), displaced beyond threshold in under the window
- **Context threshold(s) & provenance:** `zone` = `any`; `required_fields` = `['clientMac', 'locations']`; `cart_oui_prefixes` = `['0c:8b:7d']`; `displacement_m` = `30.0`; `window_s` = `5.0`
- **Evidence required:** `clientMac`, `fix_a`, `fix_b`, `displacement_m`, `dt_s`
- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend physical inspection of the cart carrying this panel and auditing the dock inventory for a cloned or spoofed panel; treat the track as low-trust until reconciled.
- **Known blind spots:**
  - cannot distinguish MAC cloning from a gross RTLS error on a single pair of fixes — both positions are inferred, not observed
  - cannot say which of the two sightings is the genuine panel

- **Severity:** high  (implemented in the engine)
- **Source stream(s):** air_marshal

### `cart_impossible_motion` — Cart panel track shows sustained impossible motion

- **Severity:** warn  (implemented in the engine)
- **Source stream(s):** dlai_track
- **Built from:** a run of Scanning-API `cloud_location` fixes for one cart-OUI `clientMac` whose segment speeds exceed the cart cap (sustained, not a single fix)
- **Context threshold(s) & provenance:** `zone` = `any`; `required_fields` = `['clientMac']`; `cart_oui_prefixes` = `['0c:8b:7d']`; `max_speed_ms` = `1.4`; `sustained_fixes` = `3`
- **Evidence required:** `clientMac`, `fixes`, `speeds_ms`
- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend reviewing the track against the flicker metric before any dispatch; if flicker is low and the motion persists, recommend physical verification of the panel for spoofing.
- **Known blind spots:**
  - a flickering track produces zigzags that mimic impossible motion — the low-trust track state must be checked first
  - all positions are inferred distributions, never observations

- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend time-boxing the containment operation and treating every position-derived alert as low-trust until it ends; the scanning radio is time-split and location accuracy is degraded floor-wide.
- **Known blind spots:**

  - positioning alerts raised during this window may be artefacts

### `deauth_flood` — Deauthentication activity reported by aWIPS

- **Severity:** warn  (implemented in the engine)
- **Source stream(s):** awips_syslog
- **Built from:** aWIPS syslog `signature`+`apMac`+`gap` (AttackAssessment kind `awips_deauth_flood`); carries NO frame count by design
- **Context threshold(s) & provenance:** `zone` = `any`; `required_fields` = `['signature', 'apMac']`
- **Evidence required:** `signature`, `apMac`, `gap`
- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend correlating deauth reports across APs by time bucket to bound the affected area; attack intensity is unknown and not recoverable from this stream — do not estimate it.
- **Known blind spots:**
  - intensity unknown: throttling to one message per signature per AP per interval destroys the frame count
  - no client identity: aWIPS syslog cannot say which client was targeted
  - the attacker's position cannot be established, inside or outside the store

- **Severity:** warn  (implemented in the engine)

- **Context threshold(s) & provenance:** `zone` = `any`; `required_fields` = `['apMac']`; `silent_window_s` = `600.0`
- **Evidence required:** `apMac`, `last_seen_ms`, `silent_for_ms`
- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend checking the sensor's PoE budget and switch port (802.3at, 12.95–25.5 W per CW9172I datasheet) and dispatching maintenance; positioning coverage near this unit is degraded until it returns.
- **Known blind spots:**

  - cannot distinguish sensor failure from deliberate RF jamming of its channel

### `probe_surge_unassociated` — Surge of distinct unassociated devices probing

- **Severity:** info  (implemented in the engine)
- **Source stream(s):** scanning_api
- **Built from:** count of distinct unassociated `clientMac`s (ssid absent) inside a sliding window over Scanning-API observations
- **Context threshold(s) & provenance:** `zone` = `any`; `required_fields` = `['clientMac']`; `distinct_macs` = `40`; `window_s` = `60.0`
- **Evidence required:** `distinct_mac_count`, `window_s`
- **Recommendation (action):** Recommend watching Air Marshal for a correlated new BSSID and treating the count strictly as a lower bound while investigating.
- **Known blind spots:**
  - unassociated randomised MACs are dropped and never reach the API (CLAUDE.md §5) — the true probe volume is undercounted, possibly severely
  - one physical device rotating MACs is indistinguishable from many devices
  - the source cannot be placed inside or outside the store by RF
